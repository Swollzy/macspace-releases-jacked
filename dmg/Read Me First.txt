Molt {{VERSION}}
================

Molt cleans up your Mac: what fills System Data, caches, big and duplicate files, apps you
no longer use, and what old versions of Swollzy's apps (Wallflower, Burrow, Swivel) left
behind. Your settings and libraries stay. Everything it removes goes to the Trash first.

Needs {{NEEDS}}.

Install
-------
1. Drag Molt onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open Molt from Applications. The first time, macOS says it can't check it: click Done,
   then open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway"
   next to Molt. You only do this once.
3. Molt asks for Full Disk Access and for its helper. Follow the steps it shows.

Used MacSpace before?
---------------------
Molt replaces it. Open MacSpace, choose About MacSpace > Uninstall, so it puts back the
settings it changed. Then open Molt. Your history comes along.
